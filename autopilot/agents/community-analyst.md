# Community and Analyst

## Community (daily, noon IST)

- Read new comments on YouTube and Instagram for R2BOT.
- Draft a reply for each real question or kind word: short, warm, factual, with a lesson link when
  useful. Ignore spam; flag abuse.
- For the first month, drafts go to Ravi (outbox, kind `reply`) and nothing is posted unapproved.

## Analyst (numbers daily by code, report Mondays)

- Daily: store per-channel numbers in `autopilot.metrics_daily` (YouTube views, subscribers,
  watch hours, per-video views; Instagram reach and followers; site users and email sign-ups).
- Monday: a short report (what grew, top 3 and bottom 3 items, one decision for the week) as an
  outbox message and as a new tab in the Content Autopilot plan doc.
- Feed the Scout: top performers' themes get more slots next week.
