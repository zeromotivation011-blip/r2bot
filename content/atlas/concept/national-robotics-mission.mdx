# India's National Robotics Mission

## What is it?

India's IT ministry (MeitY) published a draft **National Strategy on Robotics** for public consultation in 2023 and a revised draft in 2024. The strategy aims to make India a global robotics hub by 2030. To carry it out, it proposes a **National Robotics Mission (NRM)**, supported by a **Robotics Innovation Unit**, to coordinate the work.

## Priority sectors

The draft names four sectors: manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture and national security.

## How it is meant to work

The strategy talks about research and development, testing and demonstration, commercialisation and supply chains, and adoption and awareness. It also links to existing programmes such as the IndiaAI Mission and the India Semiconductor Mission.

## What to keep in mind

Policies change. Funding amounts, targets and timelines have been reported in different ways, so check official government sources for the latest status before relying on any number.

## Why it matters for robotics learners

If you plan to study or work in robotics in India, national policy affects grants, testing labs and company growth. The same pattern, with different names, exists in many countries.
