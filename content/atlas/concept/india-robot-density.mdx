# India Robot Density — Complete Analysis

## What is it?

Robot density measures the number of operational industrial robots per 10,000 manufacturing workers in a country. The International Federation of Robotics (IFR) publishes this figure annually in its World Robotics Report. India's robot density is among the lowest of major manufacturing economies.

## How it works

Density is calculated by dividing the operational industrial robot stock by the number of people employed in manufacturing, then multiplying by 10,000. South Korea leads with over 1,000 robots per 10,000 workers, followed by Singapore, China, Germany and Japan, each in the hundreds. India is far lower. Check the latest IFR report for exact figures.

## Real-world example

A country with far fewer people than India, such as South Korea, can have a much larger fleet of industrial robots. The gap helps explain why automation's productivity boost has reached Indian factories more slowly.

## Why it matters for robotics

India's draft national robotics strategy aims to grow robotics adoption and manufacturing. For roboticists in India, closing this gap is a large market opportunity.
