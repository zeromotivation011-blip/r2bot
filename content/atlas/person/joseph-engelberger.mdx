Joseph Engelberger (1925 to 2015) was an American engineer. In the 1950s he met inventor George Devol, who had designed a programmable arm. Together they founded Unimation, the first robot company.

## Achievements

He turned the idea into [Unimate](/atlas/robot/unimate), which started work in a General Motors factory in 1961. For decades he promoted robots as tools that remove dangerous and repetitive work.

## Why he matters

Without business founders like Engelberger, robotics might have stayed a lab curiosity. He is a reminder that building a robot is only half the job; the other half is finding a customer.
