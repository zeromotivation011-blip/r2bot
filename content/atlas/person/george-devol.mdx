George Devol (1912 to 2011) was an American inventor. In the 1950s he designed a machine he called "Programmed Article Transfer", a robot arm that could remember and repeat movements. He filed a patent in 1954, which was granted in 1961.

## From idea to factory

Devol met Joseph Engelberger, and together they started Unimation, the first robot company. Their [Unimate](/atlas/robot/unimate) began work at a General Motors plant in 1961.

## Why he matters

He had the invention; [Joseph Engelberger](/atlas/person/joseph-engelberger) found customers. Many robotics stories need both a builder and a business partner.
