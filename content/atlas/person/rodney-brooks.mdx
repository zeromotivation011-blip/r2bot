Rodney Brooks is a roboticist who taught at MIT and helped shape modern robotics.

## Big ideas

In the 1980s he argued that robots do not need a detailed model of the world. They can use layers of simple behaviours that react to sensors, such as "avoid obstacle" or "wander". This approach, called **behaviour-based robotics**, influenced the first Roomba.

## Companies

He co-founded iRobot (1990), the maker of Roomba, and Rethink Robotics, which built friendly factory robots. He later started Robust.AI.

## Why he matters

Brooks is also known for blunt, realistic commentary about robot hype. His advice for learners: be impressed by what works in the real world, not only by demos.
