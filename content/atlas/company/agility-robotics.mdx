Agility Robotics is an American company, spun out of research at Oregon State University, that builds walking robots. Its main product is [Digit](/atlas/robot/digit), a two-legged robot for moving boxes and totes in warehouses.

## Why it matters

Agility is one of a small group of companies trying to turn legged robots into practical workers instead of lab demos. It focuses on one clear job, which is good strategy for new technology.
