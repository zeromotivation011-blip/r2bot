KUKA is a robotics and automation company based in Augsburg, Germany. Its orange robot arms weld, paint and assemble cars and also appear in aircraft, food and logistics factories.

## History

The company was founded in 1898 and built its first industrial robots in the 1970s. In 2016-2017 the Chinese appliance maker Midea Group took over KUKA.

## Products

Industrial arms of many sizes, mobile robots, and software to program and coordinate them.

## What to take away

Robotics is a global industry. German, Japanese, Danish and Chinese companies compete, which pushes prices down and quality up.
