FANUC is a Japanese company that builds CNC controllers, industrial robots and factory automation. Its robots are easy to recognise because of their bright yellow colour.

## What they build

Robot arms for welding, painting, assembly and packing, from small desktop-sized arms to machines that lift whole car bodies. It also makes CNC controls that run machine tools.

## Why it matters

FANUC robots are known for reliability. A robot that runs for years with little attention is exactly what factories want. That reputation, not flashy AI, built the company.

## What it teaches

In industrial robotics, uptime beats novelty. A boring robot that works 24 hours a day is worth more than an exciting one that fails.
