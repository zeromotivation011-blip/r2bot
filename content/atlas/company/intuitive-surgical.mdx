Intuitive Surgical was founded in 1995 in California. Its product, the [da Vinci Surgical System](/atlas/robot/da-vinci-surgical-system), lets surgeons operate through small cuts using robotic arms they control from a console.

## Why it matters

It is one of the clearest commercial success stories in medical robotics. It shows that a robot can enter a very regulated field when it keeps the doctor in control and improves precision.
