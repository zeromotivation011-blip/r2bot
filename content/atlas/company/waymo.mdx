Waymo began as a Google project in 2009 and became a separate company under Alphabet. It builds the software and sensor systems for self-driving cars and operates ride-hailing services without a human driver in some US cities.

## How a Waymo car sees

A mix of sensors: cameras, [lidar](/atlas/concept/lidar) and radar. Each one covers weaknesses of the others. See [LiDAR vs Radar vs Camera](/blog/lidar-vs-radar-vs-camera-for-robots).

## Why it matters

A self-driving car is a very large mobile robot. It shows what it takes to move a robot safely among people: extensive testing, redundancy and careful limits on where and when it runs.

## What to keep in mind

Availability depends on the city and changes over time. Check the company's site for current service areas.
