Amazon Robotics builds and operates robots for Amazon's fulfilment centres. It began as Kiva Systems, a startup that made shelf-carrying robots. Amazon bought it in 2012 and renamed the division in 2015.

## Why it matters

Amazon is one of the biggest users of robots in the world. It shows that logistics is a major market for robotics, which is good news for learners: warehouse work needs engineers in navigation, vision, planning and safety.
