Stretch is a wheeled warehouse robot from Boston Dynamics. It has a base that drives in any direction, a long arm, and a gripper with suction cups.

## The job

Unloading a truck full of cardboard boxes is hard, hot and repetitive work. Stretch looks at the pile with cameras, picks boxes one by one, and places them on a conveyor.

## Why it is different from Spot or Atlas

Spot walks and Atlas is a humanoid, but Stretch is deliberately simple: wheels, one arm, suction. Because the task is narrow, it can be reliable and easier to sell.

## Key technologies

- **Vision** to find box edges in a messy pile
- **Suction gripping** that works on many box sizes
- **Mobile base** that moves without turning in place

## Lesson

Robots that succeed commercially usually do one job very well. Stretch is a model of a practical robot product.
