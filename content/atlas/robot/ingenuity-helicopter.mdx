Ingenuity was a tiny helicopter that travelled to Mars attached to the Perseverance rover. On 19 April 2021 it made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet.

## The challenge

Mars has an atmosphere about one percent as dense as Earth's. Flying there is like flying at a very high altitude on Earth. Ingenuity needed very large, very fast spinning blades and a very light body.

## How it flew

It could not be flown by joystick because radio signals take minutes to reach Mars. Engineers uploaded a flight plan, and the helicopter used its own sensors and a downward camera to stay stable and land safely.

## Results

It was planned for a handful of test flights and ended up flying many more, scouting terrain for the rover. Its mission ended in January 2024 after rotor damage in a landing.

## Why it matters

Ingenuity proved aerial robots can work on other worlds, opening the door to future flying explorers.
