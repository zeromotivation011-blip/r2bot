Unimate was the first robot arm used in a factory. Inventor George Devol designed it, and Joseph Engelberger turned the idea into a business. In 1961 a Unimate began work at a General Motors plant in New Jersey.

## The job

Unimate took hot, heavy metal parts from a die-casting machine and stacked them. The work was dangerous for people because of heat and fumes.

## How it worked

It stored a sequence of positions on a magnetic drum, then repeated them endlessly. There were no cameras and no intelligence. Every move was taught once and repeated exactly.

## Why it matters

Unimate began the industrial robot era. Car factories adopted robot arms for welding and painting, and the industry grew into the arms you see today.

## Lesson

The first useful robots did not think. They did dull, dangerous, dirty work with precision. That idea is still the core of industrial robotics.
