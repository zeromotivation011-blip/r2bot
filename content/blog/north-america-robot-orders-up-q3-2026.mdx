# North American robot orders rose almost 25 percent in the third quarter of 2026

A headline published on 28 October 2026 reports that North American robot orders increased 24.8 percent in the third quarter of 2026. The figure comes from industry reporting; check the original trade-group release for the full breakdown by industry and robot type.

## What "orders" means

Orders count how many robots customers have ordered, not how many are already working. Because factories decide months ahead, orders are an early signal of confidence in automation.

## What to take from it

- Industrial robots are still growing, even while humanoids get most of the headlines.
- The boring work, such as welding, packing and machine tending, remains where most robots earn money.
- Cobots, which work beside people, are a big part of the growth. See [collaborative robot safety](/atlas/concept/collaborative-robot-safety).

## What we do not know

This is a single headline. Without the full report we cannot say which sectors drove the rise. As always, one quarter does not make a trend.
