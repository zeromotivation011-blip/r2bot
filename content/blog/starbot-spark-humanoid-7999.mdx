# StarBot launches a 90 cm humanoid for families and schools at $7,999

On 4 November 2026 StarBot Robotics announced Spark, a humanoid robot about 90 centimetres tall with 22 degrees of freedom. The company says it is intended for child companionship, youth robotics education and developers, and it costs $7,999.

## What "22 degrees of freedom" means

A degree of freedom is one independent way a joint can move. More joints give more movement, but also more motors, more cost and more things that can break. For a refresher, see [servo motors](/atlas/concept/servo-motor).

## Why it matters

A few years ago, a humanoid for a school lab would have cost many times more. Companies such as [Unitree](/atlas/company/unitree) have pushed prices down, and others are following.

## A fair warning

This is information from a company press release. We have not tested the robot, and features like "open development platform" mean different things for different products. Before buying any robot for a classroom, ask for the software documentation, a safety review, and independent reviews. A $100 Arduino robot can teach the same basics for much less.
